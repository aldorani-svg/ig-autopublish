Run this as `revenue`, with `personal-brand` writing the content assets.

1. **Offer:** product, price, the reason to buy now (launch price, bonus or deadline), the guarantee if there is one, and the link.
2. **Audience and channels,** warmest first: Raw ME WhatsApp customers who opted in → IG followers → email list → TikTok.
3. **Calendar:** a 7–14 day plan, day by day, giving the channel, asset and goal for each day.
4. **Assets,** each drafted in brand voice:
   - WhatsApp broadcast: under 80 words, one link, personal, opt-out line
   - 3–5 emails (announce, story, objection, last call)
   - 4–6 Instagram posts for the queue, plus 2–3 Story frames
   - 2 TikTok/Reel scripts
5. **Targets:** units, revenue and conversion assumptions, plus what we'll measure and when.
6. Save the plan to `ops/launches/<product>.md` and add the tasks to `ops/tasks.md`.
7. **Nothing goes out without Layla's approval.** Present the assets for sign-off and list exactly what she needs to send or approve.
