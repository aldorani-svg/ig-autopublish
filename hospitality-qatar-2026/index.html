Who we are

Fueled by Raw

Raw Middle East is a plant-based food manufacturing platform in the GCC, established in Qatar in 2010. We operate from a 1,787 sqm production facility and are a 100% plant-based manufacturer certified to ISO 22000 and HACCP.

We supply retail brands, airlines, hospitality groups and institutions across the region. Our capabilities cover private label manufacturing, ready-to-eat plant-based meals, fresh and cold-pressed beverages, fruit snack programmes for schools and customised institutional catering.

Our focus is innovation and healthy nutrition, supporting growing demand for sustainable, plant-based food across the GCC.
