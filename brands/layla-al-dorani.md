# Brand: Layla Al-Dorani

**Handles:** @laylaaldorani (Instagram, TikTok) · **Site:** laylaaldorani.com. The launch post sends people to aldorani.com, so confirm which domain is canonical.

## Positioning
Evidence-based wellness, plant-based nutrition and nervous-system science for women.
Credibility comes from her IIN and eCornell credentials, Patient Experience certification, doctoral study and having founded Qatar's first plant-based factory.
She is a Qatari-American bridging Gulf food culture and modern nutrition science, which lets the brand work in Qatar, the US or anywhere else.

## Audience
Women aged 30–50, often mothers and professionals, who are stressed and want to eat better. They don't want extremes, they want a plan that is credible and doable.

## Voice
Warm, calm, approachable and grounded in evidence. "Mostly plants, most of the time." Choose consistency over perfection.
- Do: give the mechanism ("cortisol raises appetite for quick energy"), offer small swaps, ask a question to close.
- Don't: fear-mongering, diet culture, absolutist veganism, hype, overuse of emoji, or promising a cure.

## Aesthetic: quiet luxury
Warm neutrals (sand, cream, olive, soft terracotta), natural light, linen and ceramics, lots of negative space, clean serif headlines.

1. Gut health · 2. Nervous system and stress · 3. Qatari food with a twist · 4. Real life (motherhood, career, two cultures) · 5. Offers
